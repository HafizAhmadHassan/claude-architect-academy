Certification

Claude Certified Architect – Foundations

Validates that practitioners can make informed decisions about trade-offs when implementing real-world solutions with Claude, across Claude Code, the Agent SDK, the Claude API, and MCP.

Always verify current exam information with Anthropic's official certification page. Facts below were verified 2026-08-23 from official Anthropic sources listed at the bottom of this page.

Exam facts

Duration
120 minutes
Number of items
60
Item format
Multiple-choice and multiple-response items. Each item states how many answers to select.
Passing score
720 scaled (range 100–1000)
Delivery
Proctored: online proctoring and/or Pearson VUE test center
Results
Pass/fail with a scaled score (100–1,000), plus percent-correct by domain on the score report.
Fee
$125 USD
Validity
12 months from award · Renew by completing a free, non-proctored renewal assessment before the credential expires.
Question style
Scenario-based items grounded in realistic production use cases. Four scenarios are presented, selected at random from the blueprint.

Domain weights

  • 1.Agentic Architecture & Orchestration27%
  • 2.Tool Design & MCP Integration18%
  • 3.Claude Code Configuration & Workflows20%
  • 4.Prompt Engineering & Structured Output20%
  • 5.Context Management & Reliability15%
